# forge-trace

Inspect decomposition, subcalls, tool/memory hits, verification, latency, and final outcome. Classify waste: over-recursion, under-recursion, duplicate subqueries, bad retrieval, premature answer, verifier miss. Convert repeated findings into experiments, not vague prompt additions.

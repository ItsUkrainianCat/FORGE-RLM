# forge-aide-audit

Check public/private signal separation, holdout integrity, fixed-budget parity, seeds/statistics, candidate mutation paths, evaluator incidents, reward-hacking checks, generalization, progression telemetry, and pause state. Review worst candidates as well as the winner. Treat unexplained score jumps as evidence to investigate, not as automatic success.
